What it does

AI Marks Cleaner removes AI provenance marks from pasted text, plain-text files and DOCX / XLSX / PPTX files: zero-width and other invisible characters, and document properties, custom XML and add-in metadata that name the tool that produced the file. It does not touch statistical watermarks in word choice, images, audio or PDF.

Privacy

All processing runs in your browser, on your device. The page makes no requests to other sites: no CDN, no analytics. After the first load it is cached and also works without a network. The source code is public, so this can be checked.

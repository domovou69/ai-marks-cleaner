About

Removes invisible characters and provenance metadata that AI tools leave in text and Office files. Everything runs in this page; nothing is uploaded.

Options

Invisible characters (zero-width, word joiner, BOM, tag characters) are always removed. These switches change text that may be legitimate, so they are off by default.

For Office files only “Normalize spaces” applies; the other switches work on text only.

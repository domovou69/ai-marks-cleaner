AI Marks Cleaner

About

Removes invisible characters and provenance metadata that AI tools leave in text and Office files. Everything runs in this page; nothing is uploaded.

Options

Invisible characters are always removed. These switches can change legitimate text, so they are off by default.

What do these do?
Normalize spaces
No-break spaces and U+202F become plain spaces. Correct typography in Russian, so off by default.
Unicode NFKC
Folds look-alike forms (full-width letters, ligatures) into plain ones.
Replace homoglyphs
Can turn real Cyrillic letters into Latin ones. Use only for Latin text.
Strip emoji joiners
Breaks multi-person and skin-tone emoji into separate emoji.
Strip direction marks
Right-to-left text may display wrongly afterwards.

For Office files only “Normalize spaces” applies; the other switches work on text only.
